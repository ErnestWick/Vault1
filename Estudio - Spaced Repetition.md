### 🔹 Grammar: Verb TO BE & Possessives (am / are / is / my / your)

==I'm== (myself / Sarah) Sarah.
My name ==is== (singular verb to be) Julie.
What's ==your== (belonging to the person I am speaking to) name?

### 🔹 Grammar: Introducing people (This is...)

==This== (singular object/person near me) is Ben.
Nice to ==meet== (connect with / see a person for the first time) you.

### 🔹 Vocabulary: Greetings & Responses

How ==are== (verb to be for "you") you?
Fine, ==thanks== (expression of gratitude).
==Good== (positive response / fine), thanks.

It's a ==computer== (a machine used for working or surfing the internet).
It's a ==camera== (a device used to take photographs).
It's a ==bag== (an object used to carry books or personal things).
